Scanning

How do I scan something?

Tap the Scan button, point at the page, and Paperlight finds the edges and straightens it for you. Tap Done when you have all the pages, give it a name, and save.

The edges were detected in the wrong place

Tap the page in the review screen and choose Crop. Drag any corner to where it should be. Detection struggles most when the page is a similar colour to the surface underneath it — moving to a contrasting background usually fixes it outright.

Can I add pages to a document I already saved?

Yes. Open the document, tap More ▸ Manage Pages, then Add. You can also reorder and delete pages there.

My scan looks washed out or too dark

Use Enhance in the review screen and try the other modes. Black & White is usually the best choice for plain text and produces much smaller files; Colour is right for anything with a photo, a stamp or a signature on it.

Finding things

Searching inside my scans finds nothing

Searching inside documents reads the text in them, which is a Pro feature. Without Pro, search still covers titles, tags and folders.

If you do have Pro, give it a moment — text recognition runs in the background after a scan is saved. A document being read shows a small badge in the library.

My scans don't appear in iPhone search

Spotlight indexes a document once its text has been recognised, so this needs Pro and a completed scan. If it still doesn't appear, check Settings ▸ Siri & Search and make sure Paperlight is allowed to show in search.

Purchases

I paid, but Pro isn't showing

Open any Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with — purchases belong to the Apple Account, not to the device.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by Paperlight: apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions, or iOS Settings ▸ tap your name ▸ Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the period you have paid for ends.

What happens to my documents if I stop paying?

Nothing. Every document you have scanned stays on your device, opens normally, and exports as a PDF. Paperlight never holds your own documents hostage. What you lose is the Pro features themselves — searching inside scans, signatures, full-resolution export and the rest.

Can I buy it once instead of subscribing?

Yes. Settings ▸ Paperlight Pro offers a one-time Lifetime purchase.

Refunds

Apple handles all refunds, not Paperlight: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy and data

Where are my documents stored?

On your iPhone, in Paperlight's own storage. There is no server and no account — nothing is uploaded anywhere. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

How do I get my documents out?

Open a document and tap Share, or select several in the library. You can export as PDF or as images, save to Files, or send them anywhere iOS can.

I deleted something by accident

Settings ▸ Trash. Deleted documents stay there for 30 days before they are removed for good, and you can restore any of them from that screen.

Can I lock a document?

Yes — open it, then More ▸ Lock. You can also lock the whole app in Settings ▸ Security. Both use Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.
